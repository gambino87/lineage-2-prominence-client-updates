# Client alpha-0.0.14

Lineage 2 Prominence - alpha-0.0.14

Borrowed Power
- New one-time quest for characters level 15+: speak with Researcher Weissman near the Elven Ruins on Talking Island.
- Investigate forgotten soul-binding research with Karina and the Shilen Temple's residents.
- Use the reusable Minor Soul Stone on a living level 15+ monster below half health. Each monster allows one binding attempt: 50% chance to bind, then 25% chance to capture the soul when it dies. Stay alive and nearby.
- Failed attempts leave the stone reusable. Return a captured soul to Weissman to receive Soulshot: Feeble.

Soulshot: Feeble
- New equippable tutorial soulshot, with no level restriction. Grants Activate Soulshot while equipped.
- Activation boosts shot-empowered attack and spell damage by 20% for 300 seconds and costs 500 SP.
- All soul-accessory activation buffs now have a five-minute reuse time.
- Soulshot: Feeble cannot be sold, dropped, or destroyed.
- Item and activation skill use the original No Grade Soulshot icon. Updated tooltips explain activation, cost, and reuse.
- Physical soulshot hit explosions now use a lighter yellow-white tint, including critical and spell impacts. Forgotten Soldier shares this visual.

SP progression
- SP is capped at 1,000 for every job at every level. Existing balances above the cap are reduced when loaded.
- Monster SP rewards now depend on monster level minus your active job's level, instead of the monster's listed SP reward.
- Level difference / SP: +9: 80, +8: 70, +7: 60, +6: 55, +5: 40, +4: 35, +3: 33, +2: 30, +1: 27, 0: 25, -1: 25, -2: 20, -3: 15, -4: 10, -5: 5. Differences of +10 or more and -6 or less award no SP.
- Each eligible party member receives their own full SP amount. These monster SP awards are unaffected by SP rate bonuses or party splitting. XP behavior is unchanged.

Grocery stores
- Removed CP elixirs, recovery scrolls, Soulshots and Spiritshots (including compressed packages), Haste Potions, Alacrity Potions, Magic Haste Potions, and Energy Stones from grocery stores.

To apply: close Lineage II, open the launcher, and click Update. Fully close and reopen the game after maintenance.
