# Client alpha-0.0.11

Lineage 2 Prominence - alpha-0.0.11

No-Grade magical weapons
- Added Fire, Wind, Water and Earth variants of Apprentice's Rod, Willow Staff and Cedar Staff.
- Elemental variants retain the original weapon's stats, auto-attack power, cast time and recovery. They use Elemental for hit chance, school skill gains and quest credit. Base weapons remain Arcane.
- Fire autos use Fire Bolt, Wind uses Wind Bolt, and Water uses Aqua Swirl.
- Earth autos use Entangle's casting animation and Earthquake's full hit effect at 33% size. Damage remains single-target Earth magic.
- Soul Cry works with all elemental magical-weapon autos.
- Apprentice's Rod variants are available alongside the original in NPC weapon shops.

Weapon attunement
- Exchange Apprentice's Rod, Willow Staff or Cedar Staff for another aspect at the following NPCs:
- Fire: Seer Livina, Orc Village.
- Wind: Magister Vlasty, Dark Elf Village.
- Water: Magister Esrandell, Elf Village.
- Earth: Daichir, Priest of the Earth, Dwarven Village.
- Arcane: Magister Baulro, Talking Island.
- Exchanges are free, one-for-one, preserve enchantment levels and stay within the same weapon tier. Unequip your weapon before opening the exchange.

Adventurer Guildsmen
- All Adventurer Guildsmen now carry the title Guide.
- Added Ask for guidance, with advice about the guild and where to go next. Level-one characters are encouraged to return as they grow stronger.
- At level 5, guidance points you toward each race's home village, blacksmiths and head trainers to discover specialized crafts, martial proficiencies and new jobs.
- At level 10, guidance explains skill-cap quests and directs you to the five magical-weapon attunement NPCs.
- Important guidance phrases, town names and NPC names/titles are highlighted in yellow.
- Renamed Quest Information to Ask where I can find work, and Raid Information to Ask about rumors of powerful foes. Their existing windows are preserved.

Quests and trainers
- Removed 96 additional original quests starting at levels 40-80 and removed them from the Guildsman quest catalogue.
- Class-transfer quests, their prerequisite chains, subclass-unlock quests and custom quests are preserved.
- Removed the standard Learn skills option from ability-trainer dialogue, including Vlasty's separate greeting.

Soul Cry visuals
- Removed the fire ring from Soul Cry's proc impact, leaving flame and smoke.
- Reduced the remaining proc effect to 50% size. Damage and sound are unchanged.

Known issue
- Auto-attacks do not consistently resume after physical skills such as Iron Punch and Power Strike. This remains under investigation.

To apply: close Lineage II and click Update in the launcher before reconnecting.
