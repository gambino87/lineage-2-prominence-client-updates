# Client 0.1.9

Lineage 2 Prominence - 0.1.9

Weapons and crafting
- Updated weapon progression, combat values, level requirements and tooltips.
- Weapon shops now offer level-1 weapons through a single Buy Weapons menu.
- Expanded T1 and T2 crafting coverage, with recipes in Humble Beginnings and Gaining Momentum exchanges and keys in the matching village shops.
- T2 recipes use three keys worth 10% each, with the remaining material cost distributed by equipment type. Recipe scrolls are worth 10% of the finished item.
- Rebuilt dual swords and dual daggers into 61 progressive pairings each. Obtain them by combining two weapons at a blacksmith; the base fee is the dual weapon value minus both component values. Names, appearances and splitting match the new pairs.
- Removed obsolete dual weapons. Existing copies are removed during maintenance and their owners receive the weapons' base adena value automatically. No claim or token exchange is needed.

Movement and skills
- Improved momentum recovery after brief movement-key releases and corrected autorun chase turning and camera control.
- Fixed stuck movement and mouse inputs after switching away from the game.
- Heal and Chant of Life unlock at level 10; Meditate unlocks at level 12 for starting Mystics.

Launcher
- Launcher updates now take priority over client updates. Play remains unavailable until both are current.

To apply: close Lineage II, click Update launcher if offered, then Update.
