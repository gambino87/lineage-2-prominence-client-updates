# Client 0.1.4

Lineage 2 Prominence — 0.1.4

Movement and targeting
- Camera-relative WASD movement, including strafing and diagonals. Holding both mouse buttons moves forward; middle mouse toggles camera-following autorun.
- Smoother acceleration, sharper-turn slowdown, and braking when stopping or approaching monsters and NPCs.
- Tab selects eligible targets within 1500 units and inside the camera view. Targets can also be clicked while moving.
- Improved monster position synchronization, obstacle navigation, and spreading during combat. Stuck monsters can recover toward their target.
- Reduced terrain clipping when descending steep slopes.

World and client
- Added generated geodata for 125 playable map regions to improve terrain, collision, and pathfinding. Some regions still lack coverage; navigation issues may remain.
- Personal keybinds now survive launcher updates and repairs.
- Updated Ice Bolt's casting and projectile visuals.

Close Lineage II, update the launcher if offered, then click Update before reconnecting.
