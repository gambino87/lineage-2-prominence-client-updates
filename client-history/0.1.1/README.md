# Client 0.1.1

# Fighter Stances and Mystic Spell Updates

## Fighter stances and threat
- Added Aggressive Stance, trading defensive strength for increased physical attack power.
- Added Threatening Stance, increasing threat generated while active.
- Fighter stances are free toggles and automatically learned through fighter progression. Activating a stance replaces the current stance.
- Increased and standardized Spoil threat across all ranks to match the introductory Aggression skill.
- Fixed ranged parries displaying a hit effect on the attacker when no damage was returned.

## Mystic spells
- Corrected early Mystic spell progression so Vampiric Touch is learned before Curse Weakness where available.
- Expanded Curse Weakness availability to Elven Mystics and corrected Wind Shackle's class availability.
- Added Cure Poison to early Elven Mystic progression. It now restores health alongside cleansing poison, benefits from Divine skill, and has revised casting, reuse and mana requirements.
- Reworked Solar Flare into a close-range spell that blinds its target, with revised casting and reuse timing.
- Changed Dark Flare's control effect from a slow to a root.

## Spell visuals and tooltips
- Dark Flare now uses Reflect Damage's casting presentation and a purple Aura Flash hit effect.
- Wind Shackle now displays persistent wind-tinted sparks while its attack-speed reduction is active. These can appear alongside Curse Weakness's red sparks.
- Updated affected spell tooltips with consistent descriptions, effect details and class availability.
