# Client alpha-0.0.9

Lineage 2 Prominence - alpha-0.0.9

Orc Mystic: Flame of Entropy
- Added Flame of Entropy, learned at Orc Mystic level 3.
- Deals Fire damage with power 8. Uses the Enfeeblement school for training and spell accuracy.
- Base cast time: 1 second. Base cooldown: 6 seconds. Costs 3 MP; range 600.
- Applies Weaken 1, reducing P. Atk. by 10% for 30 seconds. Damage and Weakness share the Enfeeblement landing roll and existing debuff rules.
- Uses Flash of Splendor's fire pillar directly on the target, without a projectile or surrounding ring.
- Uses Fear's casting gesture and caster graphics, with Inferno's skill icon.
- Orc Mystic no longer receives Curse Weakness. Human Mystic retains it.

Spoil
- Increased Spoil's cast range from 40 to 400, matching Hate's starting range.
- Updated the range display for every Spoil rank.
- Existing MP cost, timing, success rules, and monster-level limits remain unchanged: targets must be from 5 levels below to 9 levels above your character.

Launcher 1.1.8
- Added a Patch notes dropdown, defaulting to the newest release.
- Browse older notes without changing the release that will be installed.
- Live and test-bench histories are kept separate.
- Live promotions now record their originating test-bench version. This release comes from test bench 0.2.56.

Known issue
- Auto-attacks do not consistently resume after physical skills such as Iron Punch and Power Strike. This remains under investigation.

To apply: close Lineage II, click Update launcher, then click Update in the launcher before reconnecting.
