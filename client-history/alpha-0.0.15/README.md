# Client alpha-0.0.15

Lineage 2 Prominence - alpha-0.0.15

WANTED monsters
- Five new level 13 rare monsters roam the level 11-15 hunting areas of Talking Island and the Elven, Dark Elf, Orc, and Dwarven territories.
- Each territory has one WANTED monster, with a random 15-30 minute respawn and a new eligible spawn location.
- Hunt Elyndra Thorn-Crowned, Gorak Bloodbanner, Veshra Broodmother, Rauk Ironfang, and Skittermaw.
- WANTED monsters are twice their original size, with 10x standard level 13 HP, 2x physical attack and physical/magical defense, and 200 run speed.
- XP, Adena and regular loot use 50x standard level 13 rewards, with base materials following the existing distribution rules.
- Spoil contains only the seven weapon keys used by Gaining Momentum. Each successful key roll gives three of that key; individual roll chances are unchanged.
- Get a free Wanted Flyer from an Adventurer Guildsman. View living/deceased targets and recorded death times, then click a living target to mark its current position on the map.

Mystic skills and healing
- Elven Mystic and Dark Mystic now have C Arcane and B Magical Weapon ratings.
- Human, Elven and Dark Mystics receive Heal at level 12. Human Mystic also receives Self Heal; Orc Mystic cannot receive or use Self Heal.
- Orc Mystic automatically receives Life Drain rank 1 at level 7, rank 2 at level 14, and Chant of Life at level 12. Life Drain and Chant of Life are restricted to Orc Mystic.
- Divine increases Heal and Chant of Life healing by Divine / 5 percent: 30 Divine gives 6% additional healing.
- Chant of Life is now a single-target heal over time, healing once per second for 15 seconds while preserving its total base healing. It uses Heal's 5-second base cast, 10-second reuse and 22 MP cost.
- Stronger Chant of Life effects replace weaker effects; weaker effects cannot overwrite stronger healing.
- Healing-over-time ticks and HP restored by Life Drain/Vampiric Touch now support floating healing text. Enable HP recovery combat text to see it.
- Updated Chant of Life and Life Drain tooltips, including Chant's displayed MP cost.

Fixes
- Corrected WANTED monster scaling and missing monster animations.
- Corrected the Blighted Tree Guardian's underground spawn height.

Close Lineage II and click Update in the launcher before reconnecting.
