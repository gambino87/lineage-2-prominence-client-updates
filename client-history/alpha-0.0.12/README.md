# Client alpha-0.0.12

Lineage 2 Prominence - alpha-0.0.12

Spells
- Human Mystics now start with Lightning Bolt instead of Fire Bolt. Lightning Bolt uses the Arcane school and deals unaspected magic damage; its existing power, MP cost, cast time and cooldown are preserved.
- Lightning Bolt uses the Arcane rod casting animation and a lightning impact. Existing skill slots and hotbars are preserved.
- Curse Poison cooldown reduced from 30 seconds to 10 seconds. Its tooltip now includes the cooldown.

Maps
- Corrected map-marker alignment for Dark Elf Village and 14 other towns: Aden, Dion, Dwarven Village, Elven Village, Floran, Gludin, Gludio, Goddard, Hunters Village, Orc Village, Oren, Rune, Schuttgart and Talking Island.

NPCs and shops
- Moved the Dark Elf Village Manor Manager and Gatekeeper Jasmine to their new positions. Teleports into Dark Elf Village now arrive at Jasmine's former location.
- Removed all Grand Olympiad Managers and Monuments of Heroes from the world.
- Moved Wooden Breastplate and Wooden Gaiters crafting keys from Talking Island to Orc Village's key-material vendors, including armor merchant Kunai. Prices are unchanged.
- Removed Noblesse Teleport, Gatekeeper Charm/Token Teleport and Dimension Diamond exchange options from gatekeepers.

Known issue
- Switching to exclusive fullscreen can still cause a display-device error. Borderless fullscreen is not implemented.

To apply: close Lineage II, check for updates and click Update in the launcher.

Compared with alpha-0.0.11.
