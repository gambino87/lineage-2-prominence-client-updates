# Client 0.1.7

Lineage 2 Prominence - 0.1.7

Combat and movement
- Iron Punch no longer costs MP. Iron Punch and Power Strike share a cooldown to prevent weapon-swap resets.
- Improved movement interruption and action queues for WASD, both mouse buttons, autorun, attacks and skills, while respecting casting and attack locks.
- Spoiled corpses can be selected with normal left-click and Tab.

WANTED hunts
- Expanded the level 11-15 roster to 25 named targets. Population now follows regional coverage: Orc 9, Dark Elf 6, Dwarven 4, Elven 3 and Talking Island 3.
- Expanded Dark Elf hunting coverage and separated spawn pools to keep different WANTED targets at least 2,500 units apart when they spawn.
- WANTED adena uses ten independent rolls. Hunters more than five levels above a WANTED target deal no damage and are returned to town.
- Improved flyer categories, level labels and inline status. Deceased labels change from red to orange to yellow as respawn approaches.
- Selecting a living target places a quest-style map marker; selecting another updates it without toggling the map closed. Guild Guides warn hunters about evasion magic.

World and display
- Added three custom Dark Elf hunting areas for levels 5-7, 7-10 and 11-13, plus two Elven level 13-15 areas, with random monster populations and checked ground placement.
- Corrected WANTED ground placement and added repaired geodata for affected regions.
- Added Ultra Terrain Range, extending scenery distance and loading neighboring terrain with edge stitching.

Close Lineage II and click Update in the launcher before reconnecting.


Compared with 0.1.6.
