Elven Ruins
- Reworked the dungeon with 35 encounters, each containing a roaming leader and four following minions. The raid boss remains in place.
- Leaders are larger and direct their pack's aggression. An opening hit on any member engages the pack; later attacks on the leader generate shared threat.
- Packs respawn together 30 seconds after their final member dies.
- Added 29 independent spawn points that randomly choose a Drill Bat, Monster Eye, or Skeleton. Each replacement arrives 60 seconds after death with a fresh, equal chance of each monster.
- These three monsters attack nearby players, use level 11â€“15 material rules, and count toward Gaining Momentum. Monster Eye is now level 11.
- Pack material drops follow the T3 crafting distribution. Each pack monster type can be spoiled for two different categories of T3 key materials.
- Sylph Elder and Lesser Sylph use wind-themed models.

Blacksmith quests
- Combined the regional Humble Beginnings quests into one shared quest, and the regional Gaining Momentum quests into another.
- Both quests can be started at Altran, Aios, Karrod, Sumari, or Silvera. Each blacksmith keeps their regional recipe and supply exchanges.
- Existing regional tokens convert one-for-one into Scraps for Blacksmith or Material for Blacksmith. Existing active quest progress is preserved.
- Added Restored Relics at level 15. Collect Relics for Blacksmith from Elven Ruins packs and exchange them for T3 recipes or additional crafting materials.
- All three quests distribute each token independently at random among nearby, living party members with the relevant quest active. Total token rewards are unchanged.

Crafting and trading
- Added or updated 21 T3 No Grade equipment recipes, with revised raw-material requirements and five matching key materials per craft.
- T3 recipes require Dwarven Craft level 1, cost 60 MP, and have a 100% success rate. Recipe base values are 10% of the finished item's base value.
- Fixed the broker returning to a dialogue listing screen after a sale listing. It now reopens the Broker Sale window.

Close Lineage II and update through the launcher before reconnecting.
