# Client alpha-0.0.7

Lineage 2 Prominence - alpha-0.0.7

Combat and equipment
- Fist weapons now receive Two-Handed skill gains and passive bonuses alongside Unarmed bonuses.
- Soul Cry is exclusive to Orc Mystic and cannot be used by other classes.
- Spoil only works on living monsters from 5 levels below to 9 levels above your active character class. Invalid targets are rejected before MP is spent.
- Fixed unequipping weapons and other items that occupy combined equipment slots.

Hotbars and client settings
- New characters and first-time class unlocks start with: 1 Attack, ~ Pick Up, Tab Next Target, F10 Sit.
- Mystics also start with their level 1 spell on 2. Existing class hotbars are preserved.
- Launcher 1.1.6 enables Enter Chat when applying an update, alongside disabling right-click camera reset. Original settings are backed up.

Quests
- Humble Beginnings and Gaining Momentum now show the required level in grey after the dialogue when your level is too low.
- Removed 151 original quests starting at levels 1-40, including the old tutorials. Class-transfer quests and custom quests are preserved.
- Removed those quests from the Adventure Guildsman's quest catalogue.

Quest tokens and recipes
- Humble Beginnings and Gaining Momentum tokens are worth 50 adena each for exchanges and serve as a fallback way to obtain materials and recipes.
- Token drops now average half the monster's expected adena value, including its adena drop chance. A monster averaging 100 adena gives 1 guaranteed token; one averaging 25 adena has a 25% chance to give 1 token.
- Material exchanges cost tokens equal to the material's value, rounded up, plus its full adena value. Example: a 100-adena Stem costs 2 tokens + 100 adena, before applicable taxes.
- Recipe values are now 10% of the crafted item's value (or output batch value), rounded up. Recipe token costs use 50 adena per token, rounded up.

Known issue
- Auto-attacks do not consistently resume after physical skills such as Iron Punch and Power Strike. This remains under investigation.

To apply: close Lineage II, click Update launcher, then click Update in the launcher.


Compared with alpha-0.0.6.
